cancho-ui device probe

Open on a phone, this page asks the browser it runs in the questions of issues #65 and #68 and shows the answers below, and as one block of JSON to copy. It sends nothing anywhere: no request to any other site, no analytics. It asks for no permission by itself; the buttons that do are marked, and the page writes down what each tap did. What it reports is what this browser says, on this phone: nothing here is a result until it has been run on the device.

What to do (about five minutes)

  1. Tap Re-measure once the page has loaded. Read the lines marked needs a tap.
  2. Tap the two text fields below, one after the other (tap away, and pinch back to normal size, in between).
  3. Tap Ask for notification permission, then Show a test notification, then Ask for persistent storage. Install: on iPhone Safari, Share, Add to Home Screen; on Android Chrome, the Install button below (or menu, Install app).
  4. Open the page from the home screen. Do steps 1 to 3 again there: an installed page can answer differently from a tab, and that difference is the point.
  5. Turn on airplane mode, close the app completely, open it again. The line Offline start says whether it opened.
  6. Switch to another app for 30 seconds and come back; rotate the phone to landscape and back.
  7. Tap Copy JSON and paste it into the issue, with the phone model and the steps you skipped.
